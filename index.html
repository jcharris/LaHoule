🌊 LaHoule
Interactive Marine Hydrodynamics & Offshore Engineering Suite
Jeffrey Harris (2026)
About the Suite: A zero-install, browser-based collection of interactive simulators designed for university instruction and engineering intuition. Powered by client-side WebGL (Three.js), real-time Plotly diagnostics, and physics-informed reduced-order numerical solvers running at 60 FPS on laptops and mobile devices.
